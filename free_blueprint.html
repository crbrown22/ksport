Universal Conditioning Template

Build the Engine • Beginner → Moderate Level

15 MINS MAX
STEP 1: BEFORE EVERY WORKOUT (5-7 Minutes)
Foam Rolling Calves, quads, hamstrings, glutes, and hip flexors.
Dynamic Stretches Leg swings, walking lunges, hip mobility drills.
Run Prep Perform 2 easy progressive build-ups if your training involves running.
Day 1 • High Intensity
Choose One Option Below
Option A • Repetition Run
6 × 20 sec hard run with 70 sec walk/jog recovery. RPE: 7-8/10. Hard effort, but stay controlled.
Option B • Hill Repeats
6 × 20 sec uphill sprint. Walk back slowly for recovery. RPE: 7-8/10. Focus on powerful running mechanics.
Option C • Fartlek
30 sec fast, 90 sec easy. Repeat for 6 rounds. RPE: 7-8/10 during the fast portions.
Day 2 • Interval Training
Choose One Option Below
Option A • 2:2 Intervals
2 min easy run, 2 min walk. Repeat for 3 rounds. RPE: 5-6/10. Stable, steady breathing.
Option B • 1:1 Intervals
60 sec run, 60 sec walk. Repeat for 7 rounds. RPE: 5-6/10. Excellent endurance build.
Option C • Treadmill Intervals
2 min easy jog, 2 min walk. Repeat for 3 rounds. RPE: 5-6/10. Perfect for structured indoor environments.
Day 3 • Low Intensity
Choose One Option Below
Option A • Walk / Jog
15 min continuous. Alternate jogging and walking as needed. RPE: 3-4/10.
Option B • Incline Walk
15 min continuous comfortable but steady pace on a hill or treadmill incline. RPE: 3-4/10.
Option C • Stationary/Road Bike
15 min continuous smooth, steady pedaling with moderate resistance. RPE: 3-4/10.
Pace Goal: You should be easily able to carry on a complete conversation throughout Day 3.

Weekly Training Rules & Objective

Learn movements • Establish weights • Build consistency • Prepare for progressive overload

1
Do Not Train to Failure

Priority is learning proper mechanics and building a foundation. Leave 2-3 reps in reserve (RIR) on every strength set.

2
Control the Repetition

Tempo control is vital: 2 seconds lowering (eccentric phase), 1-2 seconds lifting with power. Never bounce the weights.

3
Choose the Right Weight

Select resistance that allows you to complete the listed rep ranges with pristine form. If your form breaks, lower the weight.

4
Track & Progress

Record your weights, reps, and sets. Consistency in technique establishes your baseline metrics for future challenges.

5
Active Recovery & Fuel

Eat high quality protein (1g per lb of target body weight), hydrate with clean water, secure 8 hours of sleep, and rest on light activity days.

Conditioning Maximum Rule

For conditioning templates: strictly maintain the 15-minute maximum. Don't chase absolute physical exhaustion — chase consistency.
