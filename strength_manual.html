Coaching Key Principles
Developed by Ryan Brown, Owner & Founder of KROME Sports Performance. Perform 1-2 light feeder sets to groove the joint mechanics, followed by exactly TWO all-out working sets pushed to momentary positive concentric failure (rest 90–120s between sets). This provides optimal mechanical tension without inducing excessive neural fatigue.
4-second controlled eccentric descent, 2-second static contraction hold at peak squeeze, 2-second concentric lift. Zero bouncing, swinging, or momentum. Pure mechanical tension delivered directly into the targeted muscle fibers.
Perform an isolation movement (e.g., Pec Deck or Leg Extension) to failure, then immediately transition to a compound exercise (Incline Press or Back Squat) with 0 seconds rest. This guarantees the primary target muscle is exhausted beyond normal failure thresholds.
The protocol integrates signature KROME Sports Performance strength movements: eccentric Nordic Hamstring Curls, contralateral DB lateral step-ups, heavy bent-over barbell rows, and Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs).
Every 4–6 weeks of high-intensity overload, cycle into Day 5: Active Recovery & Tissue Reset. Features 50% submaximal blood-flow flushes, thoracic and hamstring fascial flossing, and 8-10 minutes of parasympathetic box breathing for complete cellular supercompensation.
In traditional bodybuilding, reactive plyometrics and sprint intervals are eliminated to direct 100% of recovery to muscular repair. Olympic cleans are strictly optional technique work and can be replaced with heavy barbell deadlifts and shrugs.
Feet hip-width, hips hinged back, knees softly flexed over mid-foot, chest up, neutral spine. All plyometric snap-downs and accelerations initiate and finish in this posture.
Hook grip locked, lats engaged. Push floor away without changing back angle until the bar clears knees. Sweep bar into thighs before violent triple extension and shrug.
Energy System Development blocks are 30-second continuous output intervals. Drive rotation from back hip through the thoracic spine—never flex or twist through the lower lumbar.
10-second mild stretch, followed by 5-second isometric contraction against partner's resistance, followed by a 15-second deeper relaxed exhale stretch. Accelerates neuromuscular recovery.
