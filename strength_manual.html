CNS Warmup & Recovery Guidelines

Perform these steps prior to initiating any heavy lifting wave to prime kinetic chains and prevent strain:

  • 90-90 Hip Decompression: Reset hip socket positioning and pelvic load transfer (2 minutes per side).
  • Thoracic Spine Rotations: Optimize rotation for upper trunk overhead and rotation mechanics (15 repetitions per side).
  • Ankle Mobilization: Prime dorsiflexion mobility for deep knee squats and power clean foot catch (20 reps).
  • Kettlebell Hip Swings: Wake up posterior glutes and hamstrings to fire instantly on lifts (2 sets of 15 reps).
