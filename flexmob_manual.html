Training Phases

Phase 1: Spinal & Hip Decompression

Focus: Relieve localized tension in lower pelvis, lengthen over-tight quads/psoas, and restore multi-directional hip rotation.

Routine Pose Hold Duration Decompression Focus
90-90 Hip Rotations 2 mins per side Keep spine completely tall, lean torso forward slowly over front knee.
Couch Quad Stretch 90 seconds per side Place back knee flush against wall, squeeze glute to drive hip forward.
Spinal Decompression Hang 60 seconds Relax tailbone down, breathe deeply into lower rib cage.
Thoracic Spine Openers 15 repetitions On hands and knees, rotate elbow up toward the sky smoothly.
Pigeon Pose 3 minutes per side Allow pelvis to sink into ground, keep front shin at 45-degree angle.

Phase 2: Active End-Range Joint Strengthening

Focus: Developing true neural control at maximal joint extension to prevent pulls and athletic tears.

Routine Pose Sets x Reps Tension Focus
Hip PAILs / RAILs 3 Cycles per side Isometrically press knee down into floor, then attempt to lift foot.
Ankle Dorsiflexion Isometrics 3 sets x 30 seconds Drive knee far forward over toes, keeping heel flat to ground.
Prone Shoulder Swimmers 3 Sets x 8 reps Lying on stomach, rotate arms from lower back to overhead without touching floor.
Jefferson Curl Progressions 3 Sets x 10 reps Slowly roll vertebra by vertebra holding light kettlebell or plate.
Cossack Side Squats 3 Sets x 8 per side Sink deep onto one leg, keeping opposite heel down with toe pointed up.

Soft Tissue Recovery Rules

Maximize tissue lengthening by strictly complying with these clinical guidelines:

  • Never Stretch into Sharp Pain: Target a deep, intense dull pull (approx. 7/10 threshold). Back off immediately if any sharp pinching occurs.
  • Release Jaw Clenching: Clenching your teeth sends defensive signals to your CNS, contracting muscles. Keep your lips parted slightly.
  • Hydrate Prior to Sessions: Fascial tissue requires extreme hydration levels to slide and decompress efficiently.
