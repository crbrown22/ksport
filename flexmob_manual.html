Soft Tissue Recovery Rules
  • Never Stretch into Sharp Pain: Target a deep, intense dull pull (7/10 threshold). Back off if sharp pinching occurs.
  • Release Jaw Clenching: Clenching teeth sends defensive signals to your CNS, contracting muscles. Keep lips parted slightly.
  • Hydrate Prior to Sessions: Fascial tissue requires extreme hydration levels to slide and decompress efficiently.
