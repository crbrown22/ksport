The Overall KSP Vision & Periodization Purpose

The overarching KROME Sports Performance (KSP) vision is rooted in science-based, non-linear periodization. Rather than random workouts that lead to premature plateaus and joint fatigue, every KSP protocol is intentionally mapped into distinct tactical blocks. Each phase builds systematically on the last—transitioning from neuromuscular stability and joint resilience, into progressive overload hypertrophy, through explosive velocity output, and finally into peak athletic competition or recomposition.
