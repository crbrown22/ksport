Program Phases

Phase 1: Acceleration Mechanics & Deceleration Control

Focus: Developing 10-yard burst hip mechanics, forward leg propulsion angles, and braking forces to guard knees.

Drill Name Volume / Distance Recovery Time Interactive Guide
A-Skip Mechanics 4 Sets x 20 Yards 45 seconds rest Focus on rapid shin rotation and high toe knee drive.
Wall Acceleration Drills 3 Sets x 10 seconds 60 seconds rest Maintain strict 45-degree body incline. Drive force back.
10-Yard Sled Push Bursts 6 Reps 90 seconds rest Explode low, dynamic foot contact on turf.
Pro-Agility 5-10-5 Shuttle 4 Runs 2 minutes rest Touch bottom lines, control hips on rotation pivots.
4th Quarter Lactic Sprints 6 Sets x 100 meters Use 1:5 rest timer Maintain upper trunk height, avoid slouching.

Phase 2: Multi-Directional Cut Vectors & Game Endurance

Focus: Developing high-speed lateral reactivity, curved running tracks, and VO2 max thresholds.

Drill Name Volume / Distance Recovery Time Interactive Guide
L-Drill (3 Cone Matrix) 5 Reps 90 seconds rest Cut tightly around the top cone. Do not sweep out wide.
Crossover Lateral Shuttles 4 Sets x 15 Yards 60 seconds rest Low center of mass. Prevent feet crossing over each other.
Flying 30-Meter Sprints 4 Runs 3 minutes rest 20-yard acceleration build, 30-yard pure top velocity.
Curved Figure-8 Runs 4 Sets x 2 laps 90 seconds rest Incline torso inside curve, maintain high heel recovery.
Lactic Sled Push Finisher 8 Runs x 15 yards Use Rest Timer Max intensity output to build massive endurance capacity.
